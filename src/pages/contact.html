Before getting in touch

Bring what
you have.

Site photographs, an approximate brief and any existing drawings help a first discussion. Send sensitive plans by email only if you wish to share them with the studio.

Do you only take on whole houses?

No. Hire us for the whole journey, from the first site plan to the last cabinet handle, or just for the part you need: interiors for one floor, a modular kitchen, wardrobes, a renovation or project management.

Do you plan on Vaastu?

Yes. Vaastu Shastra is part of the studio's core practice, and we balance it with the plot, the views and how your family lives.

How many design options will I see?

Proposals usually come with several options per room, from drawing rooms to dining areas and kitchens, so you can compare, choose and refine before anything is built.

Who builds the woodwork?

Turnkey woodwork comes from our own group, D.S. Doors and its D.S. Woodtech: doors, panelling, modular kitchens, wardrobes and office systems.

Do you work outside Faridabad?

Yes. The studio has worked across Delhi NCR and in Jaipur, Varanasi, Haridwar, Agra, Vrindavan, Bangalore, Bhopal and Coventry in the UK.

Is the 3D house on this site one of your projects?

The DS Concept House is an original design made for this website in the studio's language. It is not a client's property. Our real work is in the portfolio.
