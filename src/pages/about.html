D.S. Design Sense began in 2001 as Design Sense in Kalkaji, New Delhi. Today it is an LLP and the architecture and interior design studio of D.S. Doors India Limited (DSW Group), with architects, interior designers and project managers under one roof in Faridabad.

Our story

A house becomes
a home when it is
personalised.

Under the guidance of its founder, D.K. Jangid, the studio has grown into a name in architecture and total interior design, with a portfolio of national and international projects: villas and group housing, hotels and resorts, schools, showrooms, offices and temples.

At Design Sense, every design is made for a specific role, purpose or effect. We work closely with every client to plan each space around their personality and preferences. Proposals usually come with several options per room, from drawing rooms to dining areas and kitchens, so a family can compare, choose and refine before anything is built. Hard questions are settled early, while the design is still on paper, when changes are simple.

Our vision

Create beauty and bring comfort to interior spaces.

Our vision is to grow in tandem with our parent company, D.S. Doors and the DSW Group, and to deliver the finest solutions in the design and construction industry.

That means comprehensive solutions, from the first site plan to turnkey woodwork, held to six core values: superiority, quality, accuracy, passion, intensity and honesty.
