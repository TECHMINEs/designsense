A family home on three levels, designed in full by the studio, from the street elevation to the furniture in every room. The client's name and plot address are kept private.

Corner view · 3D renderB-02.1
The brief

A home for the
whole family.

Parents on the ground floor without a single stair, bedrooms for the family above, and a top floor for leisure: a home theatre, a luxury bathroom with a jacuzzi and an open garden.

A lift and a glass-railed staircase rise through a double-height lobby at the heart of the plan, so every floor works on its own and the house still feels like one home.
