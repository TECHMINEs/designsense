How to use it

Six ways into
the house.

A-06.1
Works with mouse, touch and keyboard

  1. Move around

    Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan. With the model focused, the arrow keys orbit and + − zoom.

  2. Jump to a view

    Corner, street, garden, aerial, plan or inside the double-height lobby. Or press Guided tour for six stops with captions.

  3. Change the light

    Slide from 6 am to 8 pm. At golden hour the façade warms up; at night the windows, pool and garden lights come on.

  4. Open it up

    Explode the levels to see each furnished floor, isolate one floor, or slide the section cut through every room.

  5. Try the materials

    Teak, walnut or grey stone cladding; marble, plaster or charcoal frames; clear or bronze glass; oak or marble floors.

  6. Click a room

    Every room and façade element has a card: what it is, how it is planned, and the service that delivers it. Double-click to fly in.

Site & ground

Arrive

A slatted gate, a stone path and a car porch with a green wall. Inside: a walnut pivot door, the living room off the pool deck, dining, an island kitchen, a puja room and a guest suite.

First floor

Rest

The master suite in the cantilevered marble frame, with a balcony over the street; two bedrooms behind teak louvres; a family lounge over the lobby.

Second floor

Gather

A stone-clad home theatre, a gym and study, and a terrace garden under a timber pergola, with a jacuzzi on the rear terrace.

Roof

Run

A projecting roof cap, the lift and stair mumty, water tanks and a twelve-panel solar array.
