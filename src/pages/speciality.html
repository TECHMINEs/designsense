Vaastu Shastra

A plan that faces
the right way.

Vaastu is part of the studio's core practice. It shapes where the entrance sits, which corner holds the kitchen or the puja room, and how light and air move through a home.

The grid shows the typical placement we start from on a north-facing plot. Every site and family is different, so the final plan balances Vaastu with the plot, the views and how the family lives.

Material thinking

Texture, light
and longevity.

Material choices are both visual and practical. A useful palette considers how a surface feels in daylight, how it meets the next material, and how it will age.

That is why our elevations name each finish on the drawing itself, and why our kitchens are specified down to the plywood, the hinges and the handles.
